## Observed boundary

The unchanged owned SplashDialog fixture passes its first original run,
native/Code checks and clean export, then its exported PXT crashes in generated
startup with an undefined `iface` read. The retained stack is after the image
replacement observation and before resized dimensions. A pure reconstruction
emits a correctly typed dialog but `initial` and `resized` image aliases as
`any`; Image property/pixel operations emit direct receiver member access.
This is a concrete ABI risk, not proof of the crash's exact expression.

The selected target declares Image as an interface with width/height and
getPixel shims. BaseDialog.image is a writable field, not a getter or producer
certificate. Negative allocations may yield no Image; keep those observations
and guards separate from genuine Image results. Do not apply blanket casts.
