INTEGRAL — disk-and-wheel mechanical integrator
SPDX-License-Identifier: BSD-3-Clause

Original model arrangement, radial carriage, guide structure, gravity-loaded roller,
stock motor bracket, generator and supporting design files by CrispStrobe, 2026.

The following 63 placements are adapted without changing their part poses from
ORBITAL LOOM, CrispStrobe, 2026: base, motor-A 20:60 drive, SPIKE hub mount,
large turntable, 16x16 deck and two 8x16 smooth tiles. Their original part IDs,
authorship and BSD-3-Clause notice are retained. The full original model and
notice are included in design/sources for transparent regeneration.

The radial guide uses the stock toggle-joint-on-axle sliding principle seen in
Axis Plotter / Rack Shuttle. Its positions, supporting frame and rack assembly
are newly arranged here; no source placements from those models are copied.
