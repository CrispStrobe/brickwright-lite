COUNTERTURN — SOURCE AND THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
==========================================

ORIGINAL WORK

The model arrangement, finite program, placement generator, original checks,
lessons, assembly/mechanics guides and visualization layout are credited to
CrispStrobe. Copyright (c) 2026 CrispStrobe / Christian Ströbele.
BSD-3-Clause; see licenses/counterturn.txt.

The central right-angle drive is adapted from the pinned application's
BSD-3-Clause test/fixtures/bw-sim/gears/bevel-12-20.json fixture, as credited in
the generator. The free carrier, balanced flywheel, support, mounts, retention,
physical locking control and experiment are developed for this add-on. No
external photographs or model-building instructions are redistributed.
The explanation cites the reaction-wheel principle described by NASA in
Basics of Space Flight, Chapter 11:
https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter11-2/
That source document is not redistributed.

EXTERNAL LDRAW GEOMETRY

The main model uses 23 standard rigid part types. Its physical locking control
adds one further type. Together they resolve to 273 external dependency files.
The LDR/MPD reference that standard geometry without bundling the mesh library.
The CAD illustration renders its surfaces in this original arrangement.

dependencies. Their license texts remain in their separately obtained packages;
