Original owner-supplied design. Owner-confirmed attribution and licence
supersede initial generated archive headers. Adaptation: 341 pieces. Hub pinned to its support, three keyed mount braces, two bushes replaced by half-bushes. Free ball is explicitly declared as a payload. Lift command corrected to 611.155 degrees for 64 mm.
Initial motor output angles align the keyed sockets; shaft positions are retained.
Referenced LDraw parts retain their separate authors and licences. Supplied
Python controllers and predicted animations are not bundled or executed.
